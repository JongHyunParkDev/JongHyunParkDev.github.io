Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

The short version

Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your browser. Text you paste, files you drop, images you scan and audio you dictate are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to a server we control. We operate no backend and no database.

What is stored on your device

Some tools remember your preferences using your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device, is readable only by this site, and can be removed at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

  • Interface language
  • World Clock: the cities you added and your 12/24-hour preference
  • Scientific Calculator: calculation history, memory value and DEG/RAD mode
  • Text to Speech and Speech to Text: chosen voice, language and playback settings

Microphone access

The Speech to Text tool asks for microphone permission only when you press the dictation button. Your browser sends the audio to its own built-in speech recognition service (for Chrome and Edge this is a Google service) to produce the transcript. We never receive or store the audio, and permission can be revoked at any time from your browser’s site settings.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which tools are used and how often. It records aggregate information such as pages viewed, approximate region, browser and device type. It is not used to identify you personally.

Advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent notice is shown before personalised advertising cookies are set, as required by Google’s EU user consent policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as tools are added or third-party services change. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
