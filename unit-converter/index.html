Unit Converter

Enter a value once and read it in every other unit of that category at the same time. Includes the Korean traditional units — 평, 돈, 근, 되 — that ordinary conversion tables leave out.

Most unit conversions are a single multiplication, which is why the interesting ones are the units where that is not true. Temperature has an offset as well as a scale, so 0°C is not zero anything. And several units still in daily Korean use are traditional rather than metric: 평 is 400/121 m² — 3.3058, the exact legal definition rather than the rounded 3.3 that creeps into listings — 돈 is 3.75 g for gold, 냥 is 37.5 g and 근 is 600 g as a butcher uses it. Those are the numbers people actually search for and the ones a generic converter omits.

Conversions here go through a single base unit per category rather than a table of pair-to-pair factors, so converting inches to millimetres and back returns the number you started with instead of drifting a little further each round trip.

list_alt How to use Unit Converter

  1. Choose a category: length, area, weight, volume, temperature, speed, data or time.
  2. Type a value and pick the unit it is in.
  3. Read the whole table — every unit converts at once, with the source row highlighted.
  4. Click the copy icon on any row to copy the full-precision value.

lightbulb Tips & gotchas

  • Area is the one people look up most: 1 평 is exactly 400/121 m², about 3.3058 m². A "84 m²" apartment is roughly 25.4 평.
  • 1 돈 is 3.75 g and is how gold is priced in Korea. 1 근 is 600 g for meat, but greengrocers commonly use 375 g — check which one you are being sold.
  • Storage is the classic trap: drive makers use 1 TB = 1,000,000,000,000 bytes while your operating system reports 931 GiB. Both numbers are correct.
  • Temperature is converted through an offset, not a ratio, which is why 0 °C is 32 °F rather than 0 °F.

help_outline Frequently asked questions

How exact are the conversions?

Every factor is the exact defined value where one exists — an inch is exactly 25.4 mm, a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg, and 1 평 exactly 400/121 m². Results are rounded for display only; the copy button gives the full-precision figure.

Which cup size is used?

200 mL, the metric cup used in Korean and Japanese recipes. US customary cups are about 237 mL, so an American recipe will be slightly under-measured if you use this one.

Why is 1 근 sometimes 600 g and sometimes 375 g?

The unit historically differed by trade. Meat is sold by the 600 g 근, while fruit and vegetables commonly use 375 g. Both remain in everyday use, so confirm with the seller.
