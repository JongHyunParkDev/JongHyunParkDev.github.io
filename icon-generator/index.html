Icon Generator

Turn one square image into the whole set of favicons and app icons a site needs — ICO, PNG at standard sizes, Apple touch icon, Android and Windows tiles — packaged for download.

Shipping an app or a site means producing the same artwork at a dozen sizes, and each platform has its own list: favicons at 16, 32 and 96 pixels, an Apple touch icon at 180, Android launcher icons at 192 and 512, and the maskable variant that Android crops to whatever shape the launcher uses.

One source image here produces the whole set, downscaled in the browser rather than uploaded. The reason to start from a large square — 1024 pixels or more — is that downscaling keeps detail and upscaling invents it; a 64-pixel source enlarged to 512 will look soft no matter what the tool does.

list_alt How to use Icon Generator

  1. Upload a square image, ideally 512×512 or larger.
  2. Tick the output sets you need.
  3. Press Generate & Download to get the files.
  4. Drop them at your site root and reference them from your HTML head.

lightbulb Tips & gotchas

  • Start from a square source. A non-square image has to be cropped or padded, and neither looks deliberate.
  • Detail disappears at 16×16. If your logo has fine text, make a simplified mark for the small sizes.
  • A maskable Android icon needs its important content inside the middle 80%, because the launcher may crop it to a circle.
  • Keep favicon.ico at the site root — some crawlers and older browsers still request that exact path.

help_outline Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens on a canvas in your browser and nothing is transmitted.

Do I still need a .ico file?

PNG favicons cover every current browser, but favicon.ico at the root is cheap and still requested by some clients, so it is worth including.

What source resolution should I use?

512×512 or larger. Upscaling a small source produces soft icons at the large sizes, and the tool warns you when the input is too small.
